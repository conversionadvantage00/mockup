# Greenstone Shopping Centre: website mockup v1

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step. Built on the Greenacres v6 design system (archived in `_archive/greenacres-website`).

## Pages
- `index.html`: homepage
- `stores.html`: store directory (search, 10 categories, Upper/Lower level filter, store pop-up with phone + store-specific hours)

## Brand (from their current site)
Charcoal `#313131` / grey `#58595B`, cream `#FFFDE9` background, raspberry accent `#AE415A` (dark `#772F3F`), tan `#A68661` for soft panels and the backdrop wash. Logo: `assets/brand/greenstone-logo.svg`.

## Real content used
Trading hours (incl. 37 store-specific exceptions transcribed from their hours image), levels from shop numbers (U/L), 153 stores + logos, About copy, recycling (entrances 2, 4, 5 & 7), 180 min free WiFi, 750k shoppers/month, 15 exhibition areas + 2026 brochure, management team, Padel365 Playtomic link, socials (FB, IG, TikTok), managed by Broll.
