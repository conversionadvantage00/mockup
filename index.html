Good to know

Can't find what you're looking for? Call centre management on 011 524 0445.

What are your trading hours?

Monday to Friday 09:00 – 19:00, Saturday 09:00 – 18:00, and Sunday and public holidays 09:00 – 17:00. Some stores trade longer or shorter, and each store's hours are in the store directory.

How do I find a store?

Use the store search. Every listing shows the shop number and level: numbers starting with U are on the upper level, and numbers starting with L are on the lower level.

Is there free WiFi?

Yes. Every visitor gets 180 minutes of free WiFi throughout the centre.

Where can I drop off recycling?

Separate recycling bins are at entrances 2, 4, 5 and 7 for paper, plastic, glass and cans. Please rinse packaging and remove lids.

How do I book padel or pickleball?

Courts at Padel365 are open daily from 06:00 to 23:00 and can be booked online through Playtomic.
